Standard O(n³) matmul:
  for i in 0..M:
    for j in 0..N:
      for k in 0..K:
        C[i][j] += A[i][k] * B[k][j]

Cache-blocked matmul:
  for ii in 0..M step BLOCK_M:
    for jj in 0..N step BLOCK_N:
      for kk in 0..K step BLOCK_K:
        // Process a BLOCK_M x BLOCK_N submatrix
        // where all data fits in L1/L2 cache
        for i in ii..min(ii+BLOCK_M, M):
          for j in jj..min(jj+BLOCK_N, N):
            for k in kk..min(kk+BLOCK_K, K):
              C[i][j] += A[i][k] * B[k][j]
