Phase 1 (reduce-scatter), 3 steps for p = 4: each GPU sends one segment
of its data to the next GPU and adds in the segment it received from the
previous GPU, so the combined value circulates around the ring.
After p - 1 steps, each GPU holds the fully-reduced value of exactly one
segment (one quarter of the data), not the complete result.

Phase 2 (all-gather), another 3 steps: each GPU sends its completed
segment around the ring until it has received all the others.
After 2(p - 1) steps total, every GPU has the complete result.
